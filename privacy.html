Privacy Policy

Your reflections belong solely to you.

Applies to Inksight (com.makebit.inksight) · Developed by makebit

In Plain Terms

Inksight does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no cloud servers, no ads, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Everything you do in the app stays locally on your device.

1. Overview & Developer Identity

This Privacy Policy explains how makebit handles information in connection with the Inksight Android application (package ID com.makebit.inksight) and this companion website.

Inksight is designed as a quiet, paper-first mindful journaling companion. Rather than storing your journal writing on remote servers, Inksight provides daily prompts and guided journeys while encouraging you to write on physical paper.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Inksight operates completely offline and does not collect or transmit any personal or usage information, including:

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No journal entries or handwritten notes (Inksight does not record your personal writing).
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting trackers, or behavioral profiling.
  • No advertising identifiers, location data, contacts, or device fingerprinting.

3. Local Storage & Manual Backups

To remember your preferences and reflection milestones, Inksight stores app state strictly inside your device's local application storage:

  • Reflection progress & favorites: Saved locally in user_data.json (completed prompt identifiers, completion timestamps, favorite markers, and completed guided journeys).
  • App preferences: Saved locally via Android SharedPreferences (selected theme mode, language preference, onboarding state, and currently active journey step).

If you choose to use Export Status or Restore from Backup in Settings, Inksight uses Android's standard system file picker so you can save or load a local inksight_backup.json file wherever you choose. Backup files are created only on your explicit action and are never transmitted to makebit.

4. Permissions, Sharing & External Links

Inksight does not include background network tracking or advertising SDKs. External interactions happen only when you explicitly tap a button:

  • Share a Reflection: Tapping the share icon opens your device's native Android share sheet so you can send prompt text to an app you select.
  • Optional Community Links: Inside Settings, tapping Make a donation opens Ko-fi (ko-fi.com/makebit) and tapping Suggest a Reflection opens a community submission form (forms.gle) in your default web browser. Any information you choose to submit on those external websites is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Google Play Store

When you download Inksight through Google Play, Google LLC may independently collect standard store installation, crash, or diagnostic data according to your Google account settings and Google's own Privacy Policy. makebit does not receive personally identifiable information from these store metrics.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Inksight does not collect personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region).

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Your local app data remains on your device only as long as you keep it. You can delete all locally stored Inksight data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android system settings or by uninstalling the app. Any JSON backup files you manually exported remain under your sole control.

8. Changes to This Policy & Contact

If Inksight's privacy practices ever change in a future release, this page will be updated with a new effective date before those changes take effect.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Inksight, please contact makebit at makebitcake@gmail.com.
